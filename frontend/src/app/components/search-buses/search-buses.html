Adilabad Transit Tips

  • Major Depots: Adilabad Central Bus Station (CBS) is the central boarding point for all long-distance express services.
  • NH-44 Express Highway: Direct buses to Hyderabad and Nagpur operate via the high-speed NH-44 corridor.
  • Return Journeys: Frequent return services operate daily from Hyderabad MGBS/JBS, Nirmal, and Nizamabad back to Adilabad.
  • Advance Information: Check seat counts and bus categories (Rajadhani AC, Super Luxury, Express) to plan comfortably.

Passenger Advisory

Please report to Adilabad Central Bus Station (CBS) boarding platforms 15 minutes before the departure time. Senior citizen concessions and student transit passes are valid on all applicable government scheduled services.
