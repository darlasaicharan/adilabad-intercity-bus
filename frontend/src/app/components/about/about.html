Project Overview

Adilabad Intercity Bus Information Service

The Adilabad Intercity Bus Information Service is a full-stack web application conceived and developed by Darla Sai Charan to bridge the transit information divide for residents, students, healthcare travelers, and visitors in the historic district of Adilabad.

The Challenge

Adilabad is a pivotal district located in northern Telangana, serving as a gateway between Telangana and Maharashtra via National Highway 44 (NH-44). Commuters frequently travel to Hyderabad, Nirmal, Nizamabad, Karimnagar, and Nagpur. Historically, passengers relied on inquiry counters at Adilabad Central Bus Station (CBS) or scattered notices, leading to uncertainty regarding departure timings, fares, and bus categories.

The Solution

This portal offers a centralized, digital window where passengers can instantly search bus services, compare fares, check journey durations, view intermediate stops, and assess seat availability without physical queues or third-party ticketing markups.

Transparent Fares

Visual real-time indicators and interactive seating map previews help passengers anticipate crowding and choose vacant buses.

Complete Route Stops

Detailed listings of intermediate boarding and dropping locations, helping commuters boarding at highway bypasses or rural cross-roads.

Backend Integration

REST API Endpoints

The Express.js backend exposes clean RESTful endpoints returning structured JSON data:

Method Endpoint URL Description Sample Query
GET /api/buses Returns all available intercity bus schedules http://localhost:3000/api/buses
GET /api/buses/search Filters buses by starting city and destination city /api/buses/search?from=Adilabad&to=Hyderabad
GET /api/buses/:id Returns detailed journey data for a specific bus /api/buses/TS-ADB-101
GET /api/cities Returns unique list of serviced cities for search dropdowns /api/cities
GET /api/health Health-check endpoint for server monitoring /api/health

Citizen Charter & Passenger Information Assurance

Transit Information Service by Darla Sai Charan: Adilabad Intercity Bus Information Service is dedicated to providing transparent, accurate, and accessible public transit data. All scheduled timings, approved fares, and route matrices reflect official operating timetables from Adilabad Central Bus Station (CBS). For counter reservations, concessional transit passes, or parcel bookings, passengers can visit CBS booking counters or contact the 24x7 passenger helpline at 1800-425-0033.
